# CBIO056 "HeLU-DTI: Drug Target Prediction via Deep Learning" (2023, Andy Ye) - preregistration
Written and committed WITH src/run056.py before any model is scored. In the commit chain it precedes every results commit (commit dates are author-set).
Note: this is the 2023 HeLU-DTI parent. Other documents with number 056 (2026 LLM outbreak, 2025 Alzheimer's GenAI) are different parents.

## What the abstract claims, and what this can test
Pretrained language models give drug and protein representations; a knowledge graph gives disease/phenotype/drug-effect embeddings; a heterogeneous GNN + MLP predicts drug-target affinity/interaction; evaluated on BindingDB and BioSNAP, "outperforms four baseline models"; generalisation tested by training on BindingDB and testing on an independent NR (nuclear receptor) dataset; also claims to find plausible unknown interactions. The student's model, baselines and numbers are not available.
We test a PRETRAINED-REPRESENTATION version of the idea, NOT the authors' GNN: (a) drug ECFP fingerprints + frozen ESM-2 8M protein embeddings + MLP on the public benchmark splits; (b) fair baselines and shortcut controls; (c) cold-start splits; (d) a leak-free knowledge-graph feature test on BioSNAP; (e) NR transfer if the NR data can be rebuilt from public sources. LIMITS stated up front: no drug-side language model (ECFP only), no heterogeneous GNN, protein LM is the small 8M ESM-2, the KG is Hetionet not the student's graph; "finds unknown interactions" is NOT testable and is not tested.

## Data (fixed before scoring)
MolTrans benchmark splits (Huang et al. 2020), https://github.com/kexinhuang12345/MolTrans/tree/master/dataset : BindingDB train/val/test (12,668 / 6,644 / 13,289 pairs; positives 6,334 / 927 / 1,905 - note the test positive rate is 14% vs 50% in train: a distribution shift stated up front) and BIOSNAP/full_data train/val/test (19,238 / 2,748 / 5,497 pairs; columns DrugBank ID, Gene, SMILES, Target Sequence, Label). NR: Yamanishi 2008 nuclear receptor drug-target matrix http://web.kuicr.kyoto-u.ac.jp/supp/yoshi/drugtarget/nr_admat_dgc.txt (KEGG drug and hsa ids; structures/sequences to be fetched from KEGG REST). Hetionet v1.0 (https://github.com/hetio/hetionet) for D8. ESM-2: facebook/esm2_t6_8M_UR50D (Hugging Face), frozen.
Known problems stated up front: random pair splits share drugs and proteins across train and test (shortcut risk); BindingDB train is balanced but test is not; BioSNAP negatives are sampled; Hetionet compound-binds-gene edges ARE the label, so they are excluded from every KG feature.

## Pipeline (fixed)
Drug: ECFP4 2,048-bit, radius 2 (RDKit; unparsable SMILES dropped and counted). Protein: ESM-2 8M mean-pooled last hidden state (320-d), sequence truncated to 1,022 residues, CPU. Model M1: concatenation [ECFP, ESM] -> MLP (2 hidden layers of 512, ReLU, dropout 0.2, Adam lr 1e-3, 15 epochs, batch 256, torch seed 0,1,2; reported as mean over 3 seeds with sample sd ddof=1). Metrics: AUROC (primary), AUPRC, accuracy at 0.5. Trained on official train, early stopping not used (fixed epochs), val used only to report, test scored once.

## The 10 directions (all run; gates fixed now, not re-fitted after a miss)
D1 Data audit: pair/drug/protein counts, positive rates, overlap of test drugs/proteins with train, unparsable SMILES. No gate.
D2 Headline (official split): M1 test AUROC on BindingDB and on BioSNAP. Gate "performance reproduced": both >= 0.85 (the benchmark literature reports about 0.89-0.91 for strong baselines; the abstract gives no number).
D3 Baselines on the official split: B3 = protein 2-mer composition (400-d) + ECFP -> same MLP (no pretrained LM); B4 = RF (300 trees) on [ECFP, ESM]. Gate "M1 beats baselines": M1 AUROC minus the better of B3/B4 >= 0.01 on BOTH datasets.
D4 Shortcut controls: drug-only model (RF on ECFP) and protein-only model (RF on ESM) on the official split. Gate "shortcut material": max(drug-only, protein-only) AUROC >= 0.75 on either dataset.
D5 Cold-start (pooled train+val+test of each dataset, seed 0): cold-drug (20% of drugs held out, test = their pairs), cold-protein (same for proteins), cold-both (20% drugs and 20% proteins held out; test = pairs with both held out; train = pairs with neither). M1 AUROC for each. Gate "cold-start fragile": cold-both AUROC < D2 AUROC - 0.15 on either dataset.
D6 Does the pretrained protein LM help: ESM-2 M1 vs B3 (k-mer) under cold-protein and cold-both. Gate "LM adds": gain >= 0.02 in both cold splits on a dataset (report per dataset).
D7 Distribution shift and thresholding (BindingDB official test, 14% positive): AUPRC, accuracy at 0.5, best-threshold accuracy on val applied to test, and AUPRC of a constant-score model (= positive rate). Descriptive, no gate.
D8 Knowledge-graph features (BioSNAP only; leak-free): map DrugBank IDs and gene symbols to Hetionet nodes (coverage reported); drug features = TruncatedSVD(64) of compound-side-effect (CcSE) and compound-treats/palliates-disease (CtD, CpD) edges; gene features = SVD(64) of gene-disease (DaG, DuG, DdG), gene-pathway (GpPW), gene-biological-process (GpBP), gene-covaries (GcG), gene-interacts (GiG) edges; NO compound-binds/up/down-regulates-gene edges. M1+KG vs M1 on cold-both and official split (rows without a KG mapping get zero KG vector and the covered fraction is reported). Gate "KG adds": AUROC gain >= 0.02 on cold-both.
D9 NR transfer: train M1 on BindingDB train+val, score the NR drug-target matrix (54 drugs x 26 proteins, 90 positives) after rebuilding SMILES and sequences from KEGG REST; AUROC and AUPRC vs the 90/1,404 positive rate, and vs the protein-only model. Gate "transfers": AUROC >= 0.70 AND above the protein-only model. If fewer than 80% of NR drugs and proteins can be rebuilt, D9 is reported as NOT TESTED with the coverage, no substitute dataset.
D10 Null and leakage sanity: (a) permutation null: training labels shuffled, official split, AUROC within 0.50 +/- 0.03 (3 seeds); (b) fraction of official test pairs whose drug and/or protein also occurs in train, and AUROC of a "memorise entity positive rate" score on test (mean train label of the pair's drug and protein; unseen = global rate). Descriptive except (a).

## Wording rule
README first line states whether D2 passed and which of D3-D9 fired. A positive D2 with D4 shortcut or D5 cold-start fragility firing is worded "performance on a random-pair split of a shortcut-prone benchmark; not shown to predict interactions for new drugs or proteins". No claim about the student's own GNN, no claim about novel interactions.
